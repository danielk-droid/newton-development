This page has information regarding private development projects in Newton.

How to use this page:

The chart below has a list of development projects in Newton. The projects are listed alphabetically by street name and ascending street number.

To access the information please click on the appropriate link. For additional information or if you have questions on a project that is not listed, please call our office at 617-796-1120 or by contacting the City Clerk's Office.

If you have any questions regarding the listed projects, you can email planninginfo@newtonma.gov

Other important information:

  • NewGov
  • Land Use Committee

NewGov is the online interdepartmental software used by the City of Newton to efficiently communicate and organize information. NewGov has all information regarding a development project’s applications. You can search for a project by property address to get all the records and information regarding that project. The link to each project's respective NewGov link is linked in the table below.

Link to NewGov search website: https://newtonma.portal.opengov.com/search

The Land Use Committee webpage includes information regarding meeting materials related to development projects and other information about the committee.

Link to Land Use Committee webpage: Land Use Committee | City of Newton, MA
